**Named frameworks (they make a vague skill feel ownable)**

**Authority anchor**
> "I usually charge 100k USD to deliver this keynote to corporates"
> "the storytelling formula that has helped me develop a personal brand with over 20m followers"

**Guarantee with a guardrail**
> "If, by the end of 14 days **and less than 20% course completion**, you don't feel [it's] worth the investment… prompt, courteous refund, no hard feelings."

The 20%-completion cap blocks the "binge it and refund it" buyer. Steal this.

**FAQ = objection list**
1. How long until results?
2. I'm busy, can I still join?
3. What if it doesn't work? (refund)
4. English isn't my first language?
5. Is it only for pro speakers? Who's it for?
6. Other questions

## 7. How Get Known can use this

**The model:** Content → skit/entertainment ads → low-ticket bundle → live group call → high-ticket upsell.

**Get Known version (draft):**

| Vinh | Get Known equivalent |
|---|---|
| STAGE Academy ($997) | "How to Go Viral" core course (hooks, scripting, filming, editing system) |
| STAGE Insights ($299) | Recorded masterclass: "The exact content system I use for $X/month clients" |
| STAGE Bootcamp ($299) | 30-day posting sprint: daily prompt + hook bank + templates |
| Bonus live Q&A ($499) | 2-hr live group "Content Teardown": bring your page, get roasted live |
| $199 | AUD $197–$297 launch price |

**Steal list:**
1. **One live group call as the bonus.** It's the highest-value item and costs 2 hours, and it's where you pitch done-for-you or coaching.
2. **Name every framework.** "The 3-Second Hook Rule", "The Story Stack", etc. Named = ownable = sellable.
3. **One big price anchor.** "My retainer clients pay $X/month for this system."
4. **Guarantee capped at <20% completion.**
5. **Skit ads straight to the sales page.** Test entertainment-first creative with a Sales objective, not lead-gen.
6. **Slug-per-audience landing pages** (`/bf/bundle-warm`, `/bf/bundle-cold`) so UTMs + slug tell you exactly which creative and audience is printing.

**Gap he leaves open:** A $199 buyer gets no personalised touch. A "Fast-Action" order bump (AUD $97 for a 1:1 page audit video) would lift AOV without adding much delivery time.
